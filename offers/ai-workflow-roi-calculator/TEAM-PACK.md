# Automation Team Pack — ¥98,000/month

A bounded recurring commercial offer for buyers that have already accepted at least one Automation Pilot and need multiple proven workflows maintained or expanded without commissioning separate projects every time.

## Offer

**Team Pack: ¥98,000/month**

Each activated month covers up to **5 active workflows** under one buyer relationship and includes:

- up to 2 bounded change requests per active workflow per month;
- one monthly portfolio-level ROI/health summary using non-confidential aggregate metrics;
- ordinary compatibility fixes within each workflow's existing architecture, where feasible;
- updated operating notes when behavior changes;
- one monthly workflow-slot swap, allowing a newly accepted Pilot to replace an inactive workflow;
- priority qualification for reusable components that can serve more than one covered workflow.

This is not unlimited development, managed IT, 24/7 monitoring, an SLA, or a promise of business outcomes.

## Eligibility

At least one workflow must have completed the ¥49,000 Automation Pilot or an accepted equivalent. Every covered workflow must have a measurable acceptance criterion and remain testable without posting confidential or sensitive material publicly.

## What counts as a bounded change

A bounded change modifies an existing covered workflow without adding a new system, materially different workflow, major architecture change, sensitive-data requirement, regulated/high-risk decision function, or open-ended integration obligation.

Examples include adding one output field, changing a formatting rule, updating a mapping, adjusting one bounded transformation, or modifying an agreed trigger/interface within the existing architecture.

Larger work is separately scoped before it begins.

## Commercial boundary

This page is a public offer description, not an automatic subscription or charge. Activation requires a separate written commercial agreement and payment arrangement. Either party may decline activation or renewal before a paid month if the requested work is unsafe, infeasible, or outside scope.

## Privacy and safety

Do not post credentials, API keys, personal data, employer/customer confidential information, proprietary documents, private URLs, production records, or vessel/operational information in public GitHub issues. Public qualification must use generic, synthetic, public, or anonymized information only.

## Expansion economics

The existing single-workflow Expansion License remains available at ¥19,800/month. The Team Pack is a separate portfolio offer for buyers that value one commercial relationship and standardized operations across several workflows; it does not retroactively alter any existing agreement.

**Commercial path:** ¥49,000 Pilot → accepted workflow → single-workflow Expansion License or ¥98,000/month Team Pack → bounded recurring improvements across up to 5 workflows.
