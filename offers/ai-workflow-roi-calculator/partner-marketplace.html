Automation Partner Marketplace

Need an automation workflow built, tested, documented or improved? Submit one bounded request. The request can be reviewed for a suitable independent delivery partner or a fixed-scope Automation Pilot.

No payment is collected here and submitting does not create a contract. A partner, scope, price, deadline and any referral arrangement must be agreed separately in writing before work starts. Do not submit credentials, personal data, employer/client confidential information, production records, or vessel/operations information.
