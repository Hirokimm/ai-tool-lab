## Claim-safe screening responses

The following points may be supported directly by the public samples:

- I can show a structured approach to using generative AI for workflow efficiency.
- I can show how I control AI-output quality with explicit checks and human review.
- I can show a Git-oriented method for making and verifying bounded, reversible changes.
- I can show a repeatable delivery checklist for AI-assisted work.

These statements describe demonstrable artifacts, not prior paid-client or employer results.

## HUMAN CONFIRMATION REQUIRED before any application

Do not convert the following into claims until the applicant personally confirms they are factually true:

1. Real ChatGPT/Claude work-efficiency experience that can be described without employer, shipboard, client, or private information.
2. Personal Git basic-operation/usage experience beyond the existence of these repository artifacts.
3. Sustainable availability of 10–20 hours per month.

## Privacy / employment firewall

Do not disclose employer identity, vessel information, shipboard procedures, operational incidents, internal documents, customer information, credentials, private repositories, or non-public work product. Use only fictional or independently created examples unless the applicant explicitly supplies other safe material.
