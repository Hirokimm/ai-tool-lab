# Zero-Cash Opportunity Intake

This is the public intake surface for AI Venture Factory experiments that can become repeatable, scalable, or outsourceable without using employer, shipboard, client, or other private information.

## What can be submitted

- A recurring data problem where public sources must be normalized, refreshed, compared, or monitored.
- A distribution or lead-generation bottleneck that can be measured with public/consented data.
- A repetitive workflow that can be packaged into a reusable tool, dataset, alert, or operating system.
- A buyer/supplier matching problem where a transparent brokerage or referral model could reduce search friction.

## What will not be accepted

- Requests requiring confidential employer, vessel, customer, credential, or personal data.
- Work that depends primarily on selling the operator's hours.
- Paid acquisition, inventory purchase, or other user-capital spend before a zero-cash demand signal exists.
- Illegal, deceptive, spam, impersonation, or platform-rule-violating activity.

## Submission format

Open a public issue in this repository with the title `Opportunity: <short problem>` and include only non-sensitive information:

1. **Buyer/user:** who repeatedly experiences the problem?
2. **Trigger:** what event makes the problem urgent?
3. **Current workaround:** how is it handled today?
4. **Repeatable asset:** what data, workflow, distribution rail, relationship, or IP could compound after the first solution?
5. **Economic signal:** what observable action would show real value (purchase, qualified lead, referral, signup, booking, recurring use, etc.)?
6. **Public evidence:** optional URLs or public sources that demonstrate the problem.

Do not post confidential or personally identifying information.

## Evaluation rule

The first test should be the smallest reversible zero-cash external action that can produce a real market response. Competition, uncertainty, manual friction, imperfect moat, and low initial success probability are not automatic rejection reasons. Experiments stop before execution when they require unlawful activity, user-capital spend, material employment/reputation/privacy risk, or large irreversible downside.

This page is an intake mechanism, not a promise that every submission will be pursued or commercialized.
