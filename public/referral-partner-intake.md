# Referral & Distribution Partner Intake

AI Venture Factory is testing zero-cash, low-operator-time distribution and brokerage rails for repeatable digital products, public-data workflows, lead-generation systems, and reusable automation assets.

This page is for **potential distribution, referral, data, or supply-side partners** who already serve a relevant audience or have a repeatable problem that can be solved without confidential information.

## Suitable partner patterns

- You operate a newsletter, community, directory, marketplace, or niche audience and want a trackable referral arrangement.
- You own or maintain public/consented data that could support a reusable comparison, alert, dataset, or workflow.
- You repeatedly receive a class of requests that could be routed into a standardized digital product or automated workflow.
- You supply a service or asset that can be matched to qualified demand with transparent referral or brokerage economics.

## Proposed zero-cash pilot

A first pilot should require **no upfront spend by either side**. Define one narrow audience/problem, one measurable conversion event, and a reversible referral or distribution path. Examples of valid signals include a qualified lead, signup, booking, purchase, recurring use, or accepted introduction.

Commercial terms are not assumed by this page. Any referral fee, revenue share, brokerage margin, attribution window, or operating responsibility must be explicitly agreed before a paid transaction is represented as covered by an arrangement.

## Submit a partner proposal

Open a public issue titled `Partner: <short description>` and include only non-sensitive information:

1. **Partner type:** audience/distribution, supplier, public-data source, marketplace/directory, or other.
2. **Audience or demand:** who can be reached, described without private personal data?
3. **Repeatable problem:** what recurring need or transaction friction exists?
4. **Proposed pilot:** the smallest zero-cash external test.
5. **Conversion event:** what observable action counts as demand?
6. **Potential economics:** referral, revenue share, brokerage, licensing, or other repeatable model; estimates must be labeled as estimates.
7. **Public evidence:** relevant public URLs if available.

Use the repository issue tracker: https://github.com/Hirokimm/ai-tool-lab/issues/new

## Safety boundary

Do not submit employer-confidential, vessel, customer, credential, personal, or other non-public information. Proposals dependent primarily on selling the operator's hours, paid acquisition before demand evidence, spam, deception, impersonation, or unlawful/platform-rule-violating activity are out of scope.

The objective is a repeatable supplier/customer/distribution relationship or owned workflow—not a one-off labor gig.
